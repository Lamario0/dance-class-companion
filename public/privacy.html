Privacy Policy

Dance Class Companion · Last updated November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Dance Class Companion ("the App", "we", "us") collects when you use the app or website, how it's used, and the choices you have. Dance Class Companion is a student portal for West Coast Swing class content — weekly pattern recaps, video tutorials, practice music, and class announcements.

Information we collect

WhatWhenWhy
Email addressIf you sign in (Firebase Authentication)To identify your account for features that require sign-in
Name, email, waiver acceptanceIf you submit a class waiverTo record participation waivers for the class program, via a Google Sheet the studio maintains
Attendance recordsRecorded by class staffTo track class attendance and comped entries, via the same Google Sheet
Chat name and messagesIf you post in the community chatTo show your message and the name you chose to everyone using the app. Messages are public to app users and are automatically deleted when the calendar quarter ends

We do not collect payment information, government ID numbers, or precise real-time location. We do not run advertising and we do not sell any information to third parties.

How your information is used

  • To operate core features: showing class content, tracking attendance/comped entries, and recording waivers.
  • To run the community chat. Instructors can edit or remove messages to keep the chat friendly and on topic.
  • We do not use your information for advertising or profiling.

Where your information is stored

Class content (schedules, videos, playlists) is sourced from a Google Sheet maintained by the studio. Attendance, comped-entry, and waiver submissions are written to that same spreadsheet via a Google Apps Script. Sign-in, when used, is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google Cloud). Chat messages are stored in Google Cloud Firestore and are deleted automatically at the end of each calendar quarter (March 31, June 30, September 30, and December 31). The name you post under is also remembered on your own device so you don't have to retype it. Data is stored on Google's infrastructure and is not shared with any other third party.

Videos and music from YouTube

Tutorials and songs play inside the app using YouTube's embedded player. When you play a video or song, your device connects directly to YouTube (Google), which may collect information such as your IP address, device details, and viewing activity and may set cookies, under Google's Privacy Policy. YouTube content only loads when you open the Media screen or press play. Use of YouTube content is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service.

Your choices

  • You can use most of the app — viewing class content, videos, and playlists — without signing in or providing any personal information.
  • Posting in the chat is optional. You can use any name you like; please don't post personal details such as phone numbers or addresses.
  • If you'd like a chat message removed before the quarter ends, or your waiver or attendance record corrected or removed, contact us using the details below.

Children's privacy

Dance Class Companion is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact us at lamariow@gmail.com.
